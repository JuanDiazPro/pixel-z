Pixel UI Icons Free

Documentation. 20 pixel-art icons, 16x16, each a sprite (point filtered, uncompressed, 100
pixels per unit), a folder per category:

  Icons/weapons/ (2)
    sword, bow
  Icons/equipment/ (2)
    shield, helmet
  Icons/consumables/ (2)
    apple, potion
  Icons/currency/ (4)
    coin, gem, key, chest
  Icons/resources/ (1)
    log
  Icons/tools/ (2)
    pickaxe, map
  Icons/menu/ (2)
    gear, check
  Icons/stats/ (3)
    heart, mana, star
  Icons/status/ (2)
    poison, freeze

  Demo/PixelUIIconsFreeDemo.unity   every icon by category and by name; a click shows it at 1x to 4x

Use one as an Image's or a SpriteRenderer's sprite. They are made at 1x: on a canvas or camera scaled
by whole numbers they stay crisp. The sprites import in any Unity version; the demo scene needs Unity
6.3 or later.
